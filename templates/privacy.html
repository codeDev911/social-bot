Social Bot Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

At Social Bot, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform to schedule and upload videos to YouTube, TikTok, and Facebook.

Information We Collect

  • Account information (email address and profile details)
  • Social media account credentials (with your explicit permission)
  • Video content and metadata you upload or provide via URL
  • Scheduling preferences and posting history
  • Usage analytics to improve our video processing services

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and store your videos in our secure cloud infrastructure
  • To schedule and automatically upload videos to your connected platforms
  • To generate AI-powered captions and hashtags for better engagement
  • To provide customer support and technical assistance
  • To optimize video quality and processing performance

Video Processing & Storage

When you upload videos directly or provide URLs from YouTube, TikTok, or Facebook, we:

  • Store videos securely in our encrypted cloud storage
  • Process videos to maintain high quality (up to HD for Pro users)
  • Generate thumbnails and metadata for scheduling purposes
  • Automatically delete processed videos after successful upload (unless you choose to keep them)

Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures including end-to-end encryption, secure API connections, and regular security audits. Your social media credentials are encrypted and never shared with unauthorized parties.

Third-Party Integrations

We integrate with official APIs from YouTube, TikTok, and Facebook. We only access and post to your accounts with your explicit consent and within the permissions you grant.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Disconnect any social media account at any time
  • Delete your stored videos and scheduling data
  • Request a complete copy of your data
  • Cancel scheduled uploads before they're posted
  • Request permanent account deletion

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or data requests, contact us at contact@my-port.space
